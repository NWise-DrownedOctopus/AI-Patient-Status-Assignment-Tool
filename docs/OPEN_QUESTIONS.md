# Open questions for the client (and what the tool assumes meanwhile)

The guide is a set of "rules of thumb", and a few passages contradict themselves or are unclear.
Each item shows what we encoded so the demo works, and the question to settle with the client.
Where an assumption is wrong, the fix is a small edit to one rule file.

## Medicare

1. **Fewer than 2 midnights expected and no exclusion.** The guide says Medicare inpatient needs 2+
   midnights or an exclusion. Assumed: the tool shows **Needs review** (never downgrades to
   Observation on its own). *Is that the desired behavior?*
2. **Medicaid and commercial payers.** Assumed: "Other" applies the clinical criteria only.
   *Do any other payers have their own rules?*

## Conditions that matter most

3. **Which conditions come up most overnight?** All 17 are in; we should polish and demo the
   most common ones first.
4. **Patients with more than one condition.** Assumed: the physician picks the main one. *Should
   the tool check several at once?*

## Contradictions and gaps in the guide

5. **Hemoglobin/hematocrit (bleeding).** Low Hct (< 25) or Hgb (< 8.3) is listed as an inpatient
   criterion and as an observation finding ("asymptomatic with unknown etiology"). Assumed: inpatient,
   unless the "asymptomatic, unknown cause" switch is on; any other inpatient finding still wins.
6. **Chest pain.** The intro says the patient is "hemodynamically stable (systolic over 90)" but
   the inpatient list includes "hemodynamically unstable". Assumed: unstable, or systolic 90 or
   below, is an inpatient finding. "Controlled pain" and "physician perceives coronary risk" are
   required questions; "No" gives Needs review.
7. **Dyspnea, unstable patient.** The guide's dyspnea rules assume a stable patient. Assumed:
   unstable gives Needs review (use judgment / high-risk criteria). Same for heart failure.
8. **Conditions with no observation criteria** (hypertension, infection, pneumonia, psychiatric,
   renal, pancreatitis). Assumed: not meeting inpatient criteria shows Observation with a note that
   the guide gives no separate observation criteria. *Should these instead show Needs review?*
9. **Temperature.** "PR (or equivalent)" read as rectal; entered in °F. *Confirm, and whether
   Celsius entry is needed.*
10. **Renal stone or pyelonephritis with fever (99.4 or higher).** The guide lists these as
    observation only without fever. Assumed: with fever, or with no temperature entered, Needs review.
11. **Pancreatitis.** "Amylase or lipase 3 times upper limit" read as 3x or more. Chronic
    pancreatitis is a Yes/No question ("acute? No = chronic").
12. **Neurologic deficit.** "Paresis/paralysis of extremity" is inpatient but "paresis or muscle
    weakness" is observation. Assumed: weakness without paresis or paralysis of an extremity is observation.
13. **High-risk paragraph.** The text is garbled ("occluded Superior Mesenteric Artery thrombosis"
    as the example). Encoded as a catch-all Yes/No; answering No gives Needs review, never Observation.
14. **Renal.** AKI is entered as "creatinine rise over baseline" directly; the tool does not
    subtract baseline from current.
15. **Chart-note wording.** Is the footer sentence ("generated by a decision-support tool...") acceptable
    to the hospital in the actual chart?
